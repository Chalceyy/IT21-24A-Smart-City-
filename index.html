HABAL FIND

Habal Find is designed to help users find and book habal-habal (motorcycle taxi) services in the Philippines. It provides real-time tracking of habal-habal drivers, allowing users to see their location and estimated time of arrival. The app also includes features for booking rides, viewing driver profiles, and making payments securely. Habal Find aims to enhance the convenience and safety of transportation in urban areas, making it easier for users to navigate the city and access reliable transportation options.
